## Mediocristan and Extremistan

|Mediacristan|Exremistan|
|---|---|
|Thin tails|Long Tails|
|Total is determined by many small events|Total is determined by a few large events|
|Typical member mediocre/average|Typical” member giant or dwarf|
|Tyranny of the collective|Tyranny of the accidental|
|Top few get small slice|Top few get large share|
|Easy to predict|Hard to predict|
|Impact nonscalable|Impact potentially scalable|
|Mild randomness|Wild randomness|
